2. What stays on your device

Mouvra keeps your optional preferred name, movement goals, appearance and motion preferences, onboarding/import choices, check-ins, self-reported energy, mood tags, reflections and notes, manually entered steps, movement records and guided-session completions in its local journal. Records include dates/times and local identifiers so the app can show your history. Mouvra does not send this journal to Sleepy Panda or an analytics service.

3. Optional health step access

If you choose to connect, Mouvra reads step totals from Apple HealthKit on iOS or Health Connect on Android for the current day and preceding six local days. It uses aggregate totals to show movement beside your own reflections. Cached totals include their source, covered interval and refresh time. Mouvra does not request health write access, other health data types, location or background health access. Permission is optional: manual steps and journaling remain available without it. HealthKit does not tell Mouvra whether an empty result means denial or no readable samples. Mouvra is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

4. Refresh, retention and revocation

Step refresh occurs while you use Mouvra, including app startup/resume and explicit refresh, when import is enabled. Journal records and cached step snapshots stay locally until you erase Mouvra data or remove the app; there is no scheduled automatic expiry. Revoking permission stops future access but does not by itself erase previously cached totals or journal entries. Change step access in Apple Health or Health Connect settings. After an in-app erase, imports stay paused until you explicitly reconnect.

5. Local protection and backups

The app excludes its journal from app backups and device-to-device app data transfer where supported by the operating system. Android uses the app's private no-backup storage; iOS verifies complete file protection and backup exclusion. Mouvra refuses to open its journal if required protection checks fail. This is operating-system protection, not a claim that Mouvra uses its own encrypted database or can protect an already-unlocked or compromised device. No cloud recovery or cross-device journal restore is provided.

6. Export is your choice

Export in Settings creates a JSON copy of the local journal, including cached health totals and preferences, and opens the operating system's save picker. You choose whether and where to save it. A destination you choose may be a local folder, another app or a cloud-storage provider; that provider's terms and privacy practices apply. Mouvra does not automatically send the export to Sleepy Panda. Temporary export staging is private and cleared after the picker finishes or at a later startup after interruption. Saved exports are outside Mouvra's control.

7. Erasing local data

Settings > Erase local data asks for confirmation and removes Mouvra journal records, cached step totals, manual totals and preferences on this device. It does not delete the original HealthKit/Health Connect records, exports you saved elsewhere or messages you sent to support. Reconnection is an explicit choice after erase. Removing the app also removes its local app data through the operating system. Without an export there is no Mouvra cloud copy to recover.

8. Network, website and support

The app bundles its fonts and does not download fonts at runtime. Opening the public privacy/support links launches your browser to a fixed address without journal data in the URL. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and logs visitor IP addresses for security; see https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection and https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Browser/network providers may also process the request under their own policies. If you email support, Sleepy Panda receives your email address, message and any attachment you choose to send through email providers. Do not include health records or journal exports unless you intentionally choose to share them. Support information is used to answer your request and kept only as long as needed to resolve it and meet applicable legal obligations; you can contact us about deleting your correspondence. This is separate from the device-local journal, which we cannot remotely read or erase.

9. Intended audience

Mouvra is intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults, rather than children under 13. The app does not ask for a date of birth or verify age. If a child has sent personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact us about deleting that correspondence. The device-local journal remains under the device user's control; we cannot remotely access or delete it.

10. Your choices and questions

You can skip health access, revoke it later, choose what to write, export locally or erase the local journal. No sign-in is needed for these controls. For questions or a request about information you chose to send us, contact marius.cetanas@sleepypanda.ro. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, request portability, or complain to a data-protection authority. For Romania, the supervisory authority is ANSPDCP: https://www.dataprotection.ro. We cannot use a support request to recover or remotely erase your local journal. If this policy changes, the updated page will identify its revision and date; the app's bundled policy describes that app release.
