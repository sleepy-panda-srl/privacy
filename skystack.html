Skystack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Applies to the Skystack app for iOS and Android

The short version. Skystack is a small arcade game. It has no accounts and no servers of ours: your settings, scores and streak stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which is the only part of the app that processes personal data, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland it asks for your consent first. One in-app purchase removes the ads; the store handles the payment and we never see your card.

1. Who we are

Skystack is published by Sleepy Panda SRL, [registered address], [company registration number] ("we", "us"). For anything about this policy or your data, write to [privacy contact email].

2. What the app itself stores

The app keeps the following on your device only, in its own private storage. We cannot read it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Your settings: sound, music, haptics, theme, language, reduce motion, color-blind friendly and high contrast.
  • Your best score, your streak, how many rounds you have played, whether you have seen the tutorial, and whether you have been asked to rate the app.
  • Whether the "Remove ads" purchase is owned, so the app knows not to show ads.

The daily challenge is generated on your device from the date; the app does not contact a server to get it. "Share" opens your phone's own share sheet with a line of text; nothing is sent anywhere by us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have bought "Remove ads", the app shows an interstitial ad after some rounds and an optional rewarded ad when you choose "Second chance". These ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which is software from Google LLC that runs inside the app.

To serve and measure ads, the SDK may collect and send to Google:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking), and other device and app information such as model, operating system, language, time zone and the app's name and version.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived.
  • Interactions with ads: that an ad was shown, viewed or tapped, and which rewarded ad you completed.

Google uses this data to show ads, to measure them, to prevent fraud and, where you have consented or allowed tracking, to personalize ads. Google acts as an independent controller for that processing. How Google handles the data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for transfers outside the EEA.

We do not place any other ad network, tracker or analytics service in the app.

4. Consent and your choices

EEA, UK and Switzerland. Before the first ad, the app shows a consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform). You can accept, or choose "Manage options" to decide purpose by purpose, including objecting to processing based on legitimate interest. If you do not consent to personalized ads, Google serves ads that are not based on your personal data. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings, under "Privacy".

iOS. On iPhone and iPad the app also shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the SDK does not read your IDFA. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

US states. Where a state privacy law gives you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data, the app shows a "Do not sell or share my personal information" choice through the same consent tooling.

No ads at all. Buying "Remove ads" turns the ads off, and with them the only processing of personal data in the app.

5. The in-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store processes the payment and holds your payment details; we never receive your card or bank information. The store tells the app only that the purchase is owned, and the app remembers that on your device. "Restore purchases" asks the store again, for a new phone or a reinstall. Refunds and receipts are handled by the store under its own terms.

6. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address or name is asked for or stored.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service of ours runs in the app. Apple and Google may share aggregated crash and usage statistics with us only if you have opted in to that in your device settings.
  • No data is sold. We have no data of yours to sell; the advertising processing above is Google's.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.

7. Children

Skystack is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Ad requests are not flagged as child-directed. If you believe a child has used the app and consented to ads, contact us and we will help you remove the relevant data through Google.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive the personal data about you, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Romania, the ANSPDCP). Because the data in the app never leaves your device, the practical ways to exercise these rights are: uninstall the app or clear its storage to delete everything we store; use the consent and tracking choices in section 4 for advertising; and for data Google holds, use Google's My Ad Center and Google's privacy controls. For anything else, write to us at the address in section 1.

9. Retention and security

Data stored by the app stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Google's retention of advertising data is described in Google's privacy policy. The app talks to Google's ad servers only over encrypted connections.

10. Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top, and, for material changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
