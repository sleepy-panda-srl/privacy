Optional step access

You can use manual steps and journaling without connecting health data. Mouvra reads only step totals for today and the preceding six local days when you enable imports. Change permission in Apple Health or Health Connect settings. Revoking access stops new reads; use Settings > Erase local data to remove cached totals from Mouvra.

Export and erase

Settings > Export your data opens your system save picker for a JSON copy. You choose its destination, which may be a cloud provider. Settings > Erase local data asks for confirmation and removes the journal on this device. It does not remove provider health records, saved exports or support messages. Keep any export you need before erasing or removing the app. Mouvra has no account or cloud restore.
